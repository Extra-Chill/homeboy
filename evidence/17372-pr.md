# Re-admit Cook retries on their recorded Lab runner

Fixes #17372
Refs #17455

## Root cause
Recipe-owned retries were rejected by a generic retry safeguard because successors could otherwise default to controller-local execution. Recovery could also classify an applied candidate's infrastructure gate failure as a provider retry.

## Change
Freeze the recipe's admitted dispatch in retry reservations and execution intents, preserving the Lab runner and recipe-owned provider route through normal queued execution. Continue applied gate failures rather than restarting provider work. Share retry acceptance checks with next-action projection; retain the fail-closed safeguard for generic retries without recipe-owned placement.

## Tests
Persisted Cook fixture checks that a projected retry is accepted and its durable execution intent targets `homeboy-lab`, including after reloading the reservation. Recovery unit coverage checks that applied gate infrastructure failure selects continuation. Existing Lab fixture manifests include their required workspace identities.
